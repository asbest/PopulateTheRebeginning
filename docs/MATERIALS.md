# Material sources — 11 October 2026

Four new source textures were created with the built-in imagegen tool and saved in `art/materials/`. Each source image is 1254 × 1254 pixels. These are original project assets; no textures from *Populous: The Beginning* were copied.

The final prompts were:

- **Grass:** “Create a production game material: a seamless tileable 2048x2048 photorealistic albedo texture of dense short meadow grass viewed perfectly straight down, tiny fine individual grass blades, clover leaves sparingly, subtle dry blades and hints of soil between tufts, natural vibrant spring green consistent with a lush tropical strategy game. Covers approximately 2 by 2 meters in the world. Flat diffuse overcast illumination, no cast shadows, no perspective, no horizon, no objects, no borders, no labels. Uniform detail distribution across image and all edges suitable for seamless repeating. This is a raw surface texture not a scene or mockup. Save full resolution.”
- **Thatch:** “Create a production game material: seamless tileable 2048x2048 photorealistic albedo texture of traditional golden straw/reed thatch roofing, viewed perfectly straight-on as a flat surface. Very fine narrow individual weathered reed stalk fibers aligned vertically, bundled in subtle overlapping staggered rows, rustic handmade tribal roof. Natural pale golden tan color, realistic imperfections and tiny broken reed ends, subtle tonal variation. Flat diffuse illumination no cast shadows no dramatic lighting no perspective no objects no border no text. Uniform scale across entire texture, seamless edges, raw texture only.”
- **Stone:** “Create a production game material: seamless tileable 2048x2048 photorealistic albedo texture of rustic hand built warm pale sandstone masonry, approximately 1.5 meters of wall. Irregular medium small stone blocks in staggered courses, worn chipped edges, granular pores and fine sandy mortar seams, subtle warm gray beige variation. Ancient island village architecture, original material, no symbols. Perfect straight-on orthographic flat surface, diffuse neutral illumination with no cast shadows, no perspective no objects no borders no text, seamless tiling edges, raw material texture only.”
- **Wood:** “Create a production game material: seamless tileable high-resolution photorealistic albedo texture of weathered warm brown tropical hardwood planks and hand-hewn village timber, viewed perfectly straight-on as a flat surface. Many narrow natural longitudinal wood fibers and grain lines running vertically, occasional tiny knots and tool marks, irregular warm umber/ochre color variation, subtle seams, no large boards or perspective. A believable tribal hut beam material for a strategy game, approximately 1 meter of surface. Flat diffuse overcast illumination, no cast shadows, no objects, no border, no labels, uniform detail distribution and seamless edge tiling, raw texture only.”

The tool returned 1254 × 1254 images despite the larger requested size. The game and documentation use the actual dimensions. Browser materials use mipmapped anisotropic sampling for oblique views. At the wide tactical zoom, individual grass strands average into a calm green surface, while the [browser close-up](materials-close.png) shows their detail.

The browser character meshes now use `art/character-skin-v2.png`, a 1254 × 1254 edit of the previous skin texture. It was created with the built-in image generation tool using this prompt: “Preserve an evenly lit, full-frame, tileable skin surface with fine pores; reduce orange saturation to a natural medium warm-brown/tan complexion with neutral undertones, faint freckles and microtexture; no body parts, shadows, seams, borders, text or large marks.” The prior texture remains as `art/character-skin.png` for comparison. Hair detail is a deterministic 512 × 512 canvas texture generated in `art/characters.js`.

## Terrain surfaces — 14 October 2026

The new original cliff and sand albedos in `art/materials/cliff-photo.webp` and `art/materials/sand-photo.webp` were generated with the built-in image generation tool for this project. Both were resized from the 1254 × 1254 outputs to 1024 × 1024 and encoded as WebP for compact delivery; they contain no source material from *Populous: The Beginning*. Mirrored repeating avoids hard edges on steep rock and beaches without a second sampling pass. The [terrain preview](terrain-overhaul-preview.png) shows the result in the game.

The terrain shader now uses eight texture samples rather than sixteen. Macro color variation and a subtle cavity term are calculated once at terrain vertices, then interpolated; continuous heightfield normals remove lighting seams at chunk borders. Sand darkens close to the waterline and exposed slopes reveal more rock. These changes affect appearance only: terrain positions, collisions, pathfinding and spell deformation still use the same heightfield.
