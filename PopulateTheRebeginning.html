GAMEPLAY & CONTROLS

🎯 SELECTION (How to Select Units)

Selecting units is crucial to command your tribe. There are several ways to do this:

  • Single Select: Click on any unit directly in the 3D world, or click their entry in the sidebar unit list.
  • Multi-Select: Hold the Shift key or toggle the ⊕ (Multi-select) button on the bottom bar to add or remove units from your current selection.
  • Box Selection: Click and drag your left mouse button in the world to draw a selection box over multiple units at once.
  • Select All: Press Ctrl + A to instantly select all of your allied units.
  • Clear Selection: Press Escape (Esc) to clear your current selection.

🔍 FOCUS & ZOOM

Need to find or follow a specific follower? We have a quick shortcut:

  • Double-Click to Zoom & Center: Simply double-click any unit's entry in the sidebar unit list. The camera will immediately center and focus directly on that unit!
  • Manual Zoom: Use your mouse Scroll Wheel or a touch Pinch gesture to adjust camera zoom.
  • Cinematic Camera: Press C or use Camera in the pause menu for a lower perspective. Selection, movement and spells work in both views.

🏠 BUILDINGS (How to Build)

Constructing a base is vital to train specialists and expand your tribe. Follow these steps to build:

  • 1. Select a Brave: Only Brave units (not Shamans) can build structures. Select one or more Braves.
  • 2. Open Build Menu: When a Brave is selected, building icons will appear in the action/spell bar (Hut, Tower, Warrior, Fire, Spy, etc.).
  • 3. Choose Structure: Click the desired building icon, or press its corresponding number hotkey.
  • 4. Place on Terrain: Click a location on dry land (above water level) that is close to your selected Brave. Leave enough space between buildings for people to pass. Your Brave will then walk over and build the structure!
  • Demolish: Choose Demolish in the build menu and click one of your buildings, or select the building and use its action menu.
  • Note: Each building has a specific Mana cost and unlocks new units and abilities (e.g., training Warriors, Firewarriors, Spies, or Airships).

✨ SPELLS & ACTIONS

Command the elements and guide your followers using divine spells and unit abilities:

  • Cast Spells: Select a spell (like Blast, Lightning, Raise, Lower, Flatten, Swamp, etc.) using the hotkeys 1-9/0 or the menu. Left-click the terrain to execute it.
  • Icon Names: Hold a unit, building or spell icon to see its full name. A short tap still selects it.
  • Spell Conditions: Spells require your Shaman to be alive, and they consume Mana. You must cast within the Shaman's range (which scales with the Shaman's height).
  • Move / Attack: Even when a spell is active, you can always Right-Click on the ground to order selected units to move there, or Right-Click an enemy to attack them.
  • Upgrades: Select units and click the 'Upgrade' action (50 Mana) to scale up their movement speed, attack damage, and stats dynamically.

🧙 THE SHAMAN & MANA

Your Shaman is your most powerful spiritual leader:

  • Mana: Mana is your primary resource, automatically generated over time. Use it to cast spells, build structures, and upgrade units.
  • Ancient Totems: Allied units standing beside a stone circle's totem slowly regain health. While your Shaman stands beside one, Mana regenerates twice as fast.
  • Faith: Idle followers near their own Shaman kneel and worship. Worship and healing raise your tribe's faith; deaths lower it. Faith changes Mana regeneration by up to 25%.
  • Respawn: If your Shaman dies in battle, they will respawn near your followers after a delay, unless Sudden Death is active in Match Settings.

🎮 DIRECT CONTROL & FIRST-PERSON VIEW

Want a closer look at the action?

  • Direct Control: Select a unit and use the Joystick (on mobile) or the WASD keys to move them directly.
  • First-Person View: Zoom all the way in (scroll wheel fully up) to enter First Person View and experience the world through your unit's eyes!
  • Camera Control: Drag with Left Click or use WASD to pan. Faster mouse or finger drags move farther; faster pinches zoom farther. Hold Right Click or Middle Click and drag to rotate the camera. Press Space to center camera on selected units.

⏸️ PAUSE MENU & MULTIPLAYER

Press the Pause button (⏸️) in the top-right corner to pause the game and access options:

  • Game Management: Save/Load games, toggle Fullscreen, or enable the FPS counter.
  • Campaign: Choose from 12 campaign levels, each with its own biome, unique AI, and narrative story.
  • Multiplayer: Join/host matches with PeerJS using a room ID. Terrain changes and unit commands are synchronized in real-time.
  • Customization: Adjust Match Settings (e.g. Winter Biome, Double Speed, No Flyers, Sudden Death), Tribe Configurations, and dynamic Land Generation.
