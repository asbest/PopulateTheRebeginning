CONTROLS

🖱️ Camera: Move with Left Click Drag or WASD. Rotate with Right Click Drag or Two-finger Twist. Zoom with Scroll Wheel or Pinch.

🎯 Selection: Click a unit in the world or the list. Toggle ⊕ or hold Shift for multi-selection. Ctrl+A selects all units.

✨ Actions & Spells: Select with 1-9/0 keys or the menu. Click terrain to execute, or Right-Click to move regardless of selection.

🖱️ Advanced: Right-Click or Middle-Click drag to rotate camera. Space centers camera on Shaman. Esc clears selection.

🎮 Direct Control: Use Joystick or WASD to move units. Zoom in fully for First Person View.

🧙 Shaman: Your Shaman is your most powerful unit. If they die, they will respawn near your followers after a delay, unless Sudden Death is active.

⏸️ Pause: Access Save/Load and game settings via the Pause button (top-right).
